What makes sourdough "gut-healthy"?

Sourdough contains wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria which pre-digest and break down gluten proteins, helping people with mild sensitivities feel less bloated. The organic acids formed during fermentation change the structure of starches, causing your body to absorb glucose slower, preventing insulin spikes.
